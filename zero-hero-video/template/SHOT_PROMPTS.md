# Shot prompts: "Zero, the hero inside your brand"

Paste into Luma, Higgsfield or any image-to-video model. Vertical 9:16, about 5 seconds each.
Swap `[TRADE]`, `[TRADESMAN]` and `[BRAND COLOR]` for each client. Keep the same person description in every prompt so he looks like the same guy.

**Same-person line (add to every prompt):**
`[TRADESMAN]: rugged 30s [TRADE] with a short beard, navy work shirt with two chest pockets, tool belt, [hard hat / cap]. Cinematic, photoreal, warm practical light, shallow depth of field.`

## The struggle (problem scenes)

1. **Missed calls, close-up.** `[TRADESMAN] stares at his phone, the screen full of red missed-call notifications, sweat on his brow, job site behind him, slow push-in.`
2. **On the job, phone ringing.** `[TRADESMAN] working on [a live electrical panel / an AC condenser / a water heater] with one hand, phone pinned to his ear with the other, a supervisor with a clipboard watching, frustrated.`
3. **Lunch in the truck.** `[TRADESMAN] in the driver's seat of a work truck, half-eaten sandwich on the dash, calling a customer back, tired, golden-hour light.`
4. **9 PM at the kitchen table.** `[TRADESMAN] alone at a kitchen table at night, laptop and invoices, rubbing his eyes, phone in hand, a wall clock reading 9 PM, one hanging lamp.`

## The hero (Zero scenes)

5. **Zero arrives.** `[TRADESMAN] back at work, calm now. A small glowing [BRAND COLOR] emblem pulses on his chest pocket like a hero's badge; soft holographic rings orbit his shoulder. He keeps working, relaxed.`
6. **The booked calendar.** `[TRADESMAN] glances at his phone and smirks as a translucent [BRAND COLOR] holographic calendar fills with booked jobs beside him.`
7. **Superhero walk.** `[TRADESMAN] walks toward camera through a bright job site carrying two toolboxes, confident smile, a faint [BRAND COLOR] glow trailing behind him, coworkers nodding.`
8. **Optional power shot.** `Low-angle hero shot: [TRADESMAN] stands with hands on his tool belt, the [BRAND COLOR] emblem on his chest glowing, a light wind, lens flare, triumphant.`

## Tips

- Generate 2 takes of each shot and keep the one with the steadiest face.
- Keep logos and real brand names off the generated clothing; the template adds the brand.
- Shots 2 and 5 work best when the camera barely moves, so the overlays sit cleanly.
